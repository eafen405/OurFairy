## Two judgment calls worth your review

1. **`RemoteTask.output` and `RemoteTask.report` are defaulted** (`RemoteTaskOutputWindow(0L, 0L)` and `RemoteTaskReport()`) rather than required, since the wire contract marks them with no nullability. Because `RemoteTask` is decoded out of a *snapshot*, an older backend that omits them would otherwise fail the whole projection decode and take the chat down with it — which is exactly why `RemoteChatProjection` defaults `replyStatus`, `error`, and `generation`. This matches the local snapshot-DTO convention, but it is a slight loosening of the literal contract. Say the word if you'd rather they be required.

2. **`RemoteJobOutput` fields stay required** (only `chunks` defaults to `emptyList()`, as every list field in this repo does). A direct response envelope is a different shape from a snapshot, so I didn't extend snapshot-level tolerance to it.

No contract ambiguity blocked the work; the only open question is item 1 above.
